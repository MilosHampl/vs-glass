# VS Glass — Adversarial Design Review, Round 1

**Reviewer brief:** judge the screenshots only, against the eight optical signatures of Apple's
Liquid Glass (WWDC 2025 / iOS 26 / macOS 26 Tahoe). No source, no docs were read.
**Corpus:** 22 candidate PNGs in `scratch/review/round1/`, 14 official Apple figures in
`research/reference/`.
**Measurement note:** every coordinate below is in the **native pixel space of the named file**
(`*-hero/-diff/-terminal/-notifications/-menu` are 2400×1600; `*-palette` are 1444×964;
`glass-regular-dark-sidebar.png` is 728×1594; `transparent-window-mode-cdp-capture.png` is
2880×1800). `L` = Rec.709 luma on 0–255. Pixel values were sampled, not eyeballed.

---

## Scorecard

| # | Item | Score | One-line verdict |
|---|------|:-----:|------------------|
| 1 | Lensing / refraction | **1 / 10** | Nothing behind any pane is displaced, compressed or magnified anywhere in 22 shots; every boundary is a clean occlusion cut. |
| 2 | Specular edge highlight | **3 / 10** | A real 1 px rim exists and does vary — but it is 4× brighter on the *left* than the top and effectively absent on the right and bottom, so it reads as a CSS accent border, not a lit edge. |
| 3 | Material thickness | **2 / 10** | Shadows measure ~13 px deep and ~3 L units — a token. The one strong edge (terminal panel) is a hard 8 px black groove, i.e. an engraved line, not a slab. |
| 4 | Vibrancy | **2 / 10** | Foreground colours are fixed hex throughout; identical syntax colours in the near-black editor and the 2× lighter sidebar, and no saturation boost anywhere. |
| 5 | Floating layered panes | **2 / 10** | Everything is butted flat. In light mode sidebar and editor differ by ΔL = 3.4 (1.04 : 1) with a hairline between them. Only the transparent-mode capture shows real gaps — and it contradicts every other shot. |
| 6 | Concentric geometry | **2 / 10** | Radii are 0 (sidebar), 20 (palette), ~7 (hover), ~12 (suggest), ~6 (toast) with **14 px children inside the 6 px toast** — child radius exceeds parent radius, the exact inverse of Apple's rule. |
| 7 | Adaptive tint | **4 / 10** | The best-performing item: real hue ramps exist (clear sidebar swings #1e2738 → #1e3556 top-to-bottom). But the hue is generated by the theme, not sampled from anything behind, and in light mode it is dead neutral. |
| 8 | Liquid response | **1 / 10** | The only state change visible across shots is a hard saturated blue 2 px focus ring switching on. No highlight travel, no flex, no damping cue. |

**Composite: ~2.1 / 10 against the Liquid Glass spec.** As a dark VS Code theme it is competent and
legible. As a reproduction of Liquid Glass optics it does not clear the bar in a single category.

## The finding that frames everything else

`glass-regular-dark-hero.png` and `glass-regular-dark-layer1-hero.png` are the same theme *with*
and *without* the entire effects layer. **79.3 % of pixels differ by ≤ 5/255; 71.8 % by ≤ 2/255** —
and part of even that is editor content drift (breadcrumb, selection, cursor). Sampling identical
regions:

| Region | with effects | layer1 (no effects) | Δ L |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sidebar body (400, 1100) | `#272c38` L 43.8 | `#272b34` L 42.8 | **1.0** |
| Editor body (1600, 1400) | `#13161f` L 22.0 | `#14171e` L 22.9 | **0.9** |
| Activity bar (40, 900) | `#1d222d` L 33.7 | `#1f232b` L 34.7 | **1.0** |
| Title bar (1200, 20) | `#282e3a` L 45.6 | `#1d212a` L 32.8 | 12.8 |
| Tab strip (1700, 105) | `#50545b` L 83.7 | `#43464c` L 69.8 | 13.9 |
| Status bar (800, 1570) | `#1e2b41` L 41.8 | `#1f232c` L 34.8 | 7.0 |

The effects layer measurably touches the title bar, tab strip and status bar and does **essentially
nothing to the two largest surfaces in the product** — the sidebar and the editor. Add
`glass-opaque-layer1-hero.png`, where activity bar, sidebar and status bar are *literally the same
hex* (`#1c2129`), and three of the four shipped variants are indistinguishable at arm's length. A
reviewer handed these four files unlabelled could not sort them.

## Per-item findings

### 1. Lensing / refraction — 1 / 10

This is the signature and it is simply not implemented. Every boundary in the corpus is a hard
occlusion cut with zero displacement of what lies behind.

- `glass-regular-dark-hover.png`: the tooltip's left edge lands at x ≈ 130 and slices the code
  behind it mid-word — `…ith type`, `…rface Ma`, `…name: st`. The glyphs immediately outside the
  boundary sit exactly where they would with no pane present. Compare Apple's
  `07-hig-liquid-glass-components-illustration.png`: the blue slider track passing *behind* the
  toggle knob visibly bows, thickens and bleeds colour around the knob's rim, and the rainbow
  slider under the glass pill is bent into an S. That bending is the whole illusion. Here the
  tooltip is an opaque grey card sitting on top of text.
- `glass-regular-dark-palette.png`: nothing of the editor visible at x < 130 reappears inside the
  panel at a displaced y. The panel is not a lens; it is a lid.
- `glass-regular-dark-suggest.png`: the "No suggestions." pill has the nicest radius in the set,
  and `alProperties {` behind it terminates dead at the pill boundary. No compression at the
  capsule ends, where an actual lens would show the strongest displacement.
- `zoom-palette-rim-bottom-left.png` was presumably captured to prove this point and instead shows
  the region *below* the palette, ~2/3 of it pure `#000000` off-canvas. It contains no rim.

The failure is total, so the score is 1 rather than 0 only because nothing about the geometry
forecloses adding it later.

### 2. Specular edge highlight — 3 / 10

There *is* a rim, it is a consistent 2 device px (1 CSS px), and it does vary — but the variation
is the wrong shape and two of four edges are missing. Measured on `glass-regular-dark-palette.png`
at mid-pane:

| Edge | rim L | adjacent interior L | Δ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Left (x 122–123, y 400) | 176.7 | 66.9 | **+110** |
| Top (y 14–15, x 700) | 127.4 | 112.5 | **+15** |
| Bottom (y 732–733, x 700) | 65.6 | 58.1 | **+7** |
| Right (x 1320–1321, y 400) | 61.3 | 57.4 | **+4** |

A virtual light in Apple's material sits top-leading: the top edge is brightest, the leading edge
nearly as bright, both decaying smoothly *around the corner arc*, with a faint bounce line at the
bottom and a whisper on the trailing edge — the pane never loses its outline. Look at
`11-adoption-tabbar-liquid-glass-after-dark.png`: the pill's rim is continuous through 360° and
brightest on the upper-left shoulder. Here the pane is lit almost side-on from the far left and
loses its rim entirely on two sides, which is why the palette's bottom-right corner dissolves into
the editor.

On the sidebar the profile is worse. Scanning the left rim of `glass-regular-dark-sidebar.png`
down its full height (x = 66):

```
y=  70  L= 89.3   |   y= 670  L= 95.9   |   y=1170  L= 84.4
y= 170  L= 80.5   |   y= 770  L= 91.9   |   y=1270  L= 82.6
y= 270  L=105.1   |   y= 870  L= 87.8   |   y=1370  L= 83.1
y= 370  L=134.2 <-|   y= 970  L= 85.5   |   y=1470  L= 82.4
y= 470  L=114.7   |   y=1070  L= 84.6   |
```

The peak is at y ≈ 370 — a quarter of the way down, coincident with the selected `glass.ts` row —
and the **top of the rim (y = 70–170) is among the dimmest points on it**. A top-lit edge cannot be
darkest at the top. And the horizontal top rim reads L 104.8 against a header of 78.1 (Δ +27) while
the left rim at the same corner is Δ +85: the two edges that meet at the same corner are lit by
different lights.

### 3. Material thickness — 2 / 10

Neither the outer shadow nor the inner shadow is doing real work.

- **Outer shadow, palette (dark):** below the bottom rim, L runs 13.4 → 16.2 over 13 px before
  reaching ambient. That is a 13 px, ~3-unit shadow — invisible at 100 %. To the right it is the
  same: 15.9 → 19.2 over 15 px. Apple's overlays cast a wide, soft, clearly readable penumbra;
  `08-adoption-sidebar-background-extension-light.png` shows the sidebar's shadow spreading tens of
  points into the content. The light palette (`glass-regular-light-palette.png`) is the one shot
  where a shadow is actually legible, which proves the recipe exists and is under-dialled in dark.
- **Inner shadow:** absent. What exists instead is *banding*. Inside the palette's left rim there is
  a 10 px lighter strip (x 124–133, L ≈ 67) that terminates at x = 134 in a hard step down to
  L 58.4. That is a second parallel edge line, not a gradient — visible as a faint double border.
- **The terminal panel is worse.** In `glass-regular-dark-terminal.png` at x = 1400 the boundary
  reads: editor content, then y 1012–1019 an **8 px band at L ≈ 12.5 (near black)**, then y 1022–1023
  a bright rim at L 77, then panel interior at L 50. A hard black groove plus a hard bright line is
  a *bevel* — a carved-in edge from 2005 skeuomorphism. Glass casts a soft shadow onto what is
  behind it; it does not incise a channel.
- **`glass-regular-dark-notifications.png` inverts the physics.** Scanning left of the toast at
  y = 1400: editor L 44.7, then x 1473–1479 a **bright halo ramping L 124.9 → 162.5**, then the rim
  at L 190, then interior L 81. The toast *emits light outward*. Glass does the opposite. This is
  the single most un-Apple pixel in the corpus.

### 4. Vibrancy — 2 / 10

Foreground colour is fixed hex everywhere and does not respond to the material under it.

- The sidebar body sits at L 43.8 and the editor at L 22.0 — a 2× luminance difference — yet
  identical token colours are used on both. Apple's `04-materials-legibility-vibrant-label.png` vs
  `05-…-nonvibrant-label.png` is precisely this comparison, and the theme has shipped the *negative*
  example: flat colour on a variable material.
- No saturation boost. In `glass-clear-hero.png` the status bar sits over a strongly chromatic
  `#143765` ground and the status text stays the same neutral white it is in
  `glass-opaque-layer1-hero.png` over `#1c2129`.
- Separators are flat 1 px rules, not vibrant: the Outline/Timeline dividers in
  `glass-regular-dark-sidebar.png` (y ≈ 1405, 1465) run pane-edge to pane-edge at a constant value.
- Where the material *does* get thin, legibility breaks instead of vibrancy saving it — see
  `glass-clear-palette.png`, item 10 below.

Two points, not zero, because the icon colours in the file tree (React blue, Python blue/yellow,
SQL magenta) are lively and the palette match-highlight blue is at least a deliberate accent.

### 5. Floating layered panes — 2 / 10

Nothing floats. The window is a set of butted rectangles sharing hairline edges.

- **Light mode is the proof.** `glass-regular-light-hero.png`: sidebar `#f3f7ff` (L 244.7) against
  editor `#f9fafe` (L 248.1) — **ΔL 3.4, a 1.04 : 1 contrast ratio.** The two panes are the same
  surface with a line drawn on it. Apple's light sidebar in
  `08-adoption-sidebar-background-extension-light.png` is unmistakably a separate slab inset from
  every window edge, with a radius, a rim and a shadow.
- **Apple's sidebar is markedly more opaque than its toolbar** (compare the sidebar to the floating
  back-chevron button in figures 08/09). Here the relationship is undifferentiated: sidebar, panel,
  title bar and status bar all read as one opacity family.
- The **activity-bar / sidebar seam** is the worst joint. In `glass-regular-dark-sidebar.png` at
  y = 200 the pixels run `…2f3563 303665 | 4b4f5b 4b4f5b | 2e3240…` — a dark groove at x 62–63, a
  lighter purple step at x 64–65, the rim at x 66–67, then the body. Four distinct values in six
  pixels: a double border. The activity bar's top button is also **clipped by the window's left
  edge**, its left radius running off-canvas.
- The **status bar** has no separation at all: in `glass-regular-dark-hero.png` it shares the
  window ground and is delimited only by a value change.
- **The transparent-window capture contradicts the rest of the product.** In
  `transparent-window-mode-cdp-capture.png` the sidebar genuinely has rounded corners (≈ 10 px),
  there is a real ~13 px gap between the sidebar's right edge (x ≈ 477) and the editor's left edge
  (x ≈ 490), and the window itself is radiused. That is the layout the other 21 shots should have.
  It is also, per the brief, the shot where transparent regions render white — and **there is no
  white anywhere in it**. Either nothing is actually transparent, or the capture composited against
  a dark backdrop. Either way the single most important demo for this product currently proves
  nothing.

### 6. Concentric geometry — 2 / 10

Radii are inconsistent between panes and inverted between parents and children.

- **Sidebar radius is 0.** Scanning `glass-regular-dark-hero.png` for the first bright pixel per row
  from y = 74 to y = 108, the left edge sits at x = 92–96 for *every* row — no arc. Same at the
  bottom (y = 1520 → 1532). Confirmed in the sidebar crop, where the horizontal top rim and the
  vertical left rim meet at a right angle at (66, 66). Apple's sidebars run ~26 pt.
- **Palette radius is 20 px** (corner arc measured from x = 140 at y = 12 to x = 120 at y = 32) on a
  1204 × 725 panel — modest for the size, and unrelated to any other radius in the theme.
- **The notification toast is the clearest violation.** Its own corners are ~6 px, its
  Yes / Always / Never buttons are ~14 px pills. **The children are more rounded than the parent.**
  Apple's rule is the inverse: child radius = parent radius − inset, which is exactly what
  `11-adoption-tabbar-liquid-glass-after-dark.png` shows (selected pill nested concentrically inside
  the bar) and what the concentric construction circles in figure 07 are drawing.
- **The light palette's selected row is square-cornered and full-bleed** inside a 20 px-rounded
  parent, so at (135, 95) the flat blue bar collides with the container's corner arc.
- The hover tooltip carries a hard vertical divider at x ≈ 167 running edge to edge through the
  rounded corner zone, splitting one slab into two tones.

### 7. Adaptive tint — 4 / 10

The strongest item, and the only one with a real mechanism visible.

- `glass-clear-hero.png` sidebar: `#1e2738` (L 38.3) at y = 1100 → `#1e3556` (L 50.5) at y = 1500.
  A genuine hue-and-value ramp. The status bar picks up `#143765`. The dark sidebar shows a
  purple-at-top / teal-at-middle / blue-at-bottom drift.
- But the tint is **generated, not sampled**. Nothing is behind these panes, so the wash has no
  referent; the mid-height teal in the dark sidebar reads as a colour clash against the purple
  header band rather than as the material picking anything up.
- The clear variant's ground is a **four-corner mesh** — TL `#2a46bb` (L 72.5), TR `#15485c`,
  BR `#382b85`, BL `#142e69`, but top-centre and bottom-centre both `#141d3b` (L 29.3). Corners
  brighter than mid-edges reads as decorative wallpaper, not as a material response.
- **Clear does not deliver its brief.** Apple's caption for `03-materials-liquid-glass-clear-variant.png`
  is that Clear "allows the visual detail of the background beneath it to show through" — the brick
  courses are plainly readable through the disc. Here Clear differs from Regular only in the
  saturation of a ~10 px strip at the window edge; the sidebar body is still near-opaque.
- **Light mode has no tint at all** — `#f3f7ff` / `#f9fafe`, functionally neutral. Apple's
  `01-materials-liquid-glass-regular-over-light.png` carries an obvious warm/blue cast from the
  beach behind it.

### 8. Liquid response — 1 / 10

Judged from the state evidence available:

- Selected sidebar row: `glass-regular-dark-hero.png` shows a plain fill; `…-sidebar.png` and
  `glass-clear-terminal.png` show the same row with a **hard 2 px saturated blue ring**. A binary
  stroke toggle is the opposite of a damped material flex.
- The palette input carries the same hard blue focus ring (`glass-regular-light-palette.png`,
  ~`#2f7fd8`, 2 px, uniform) — stock VS Code focus, unretouched.
- Between `glass-regular-dark-hover.png` and its layer1 twin, the effects layer adds a radius and a
  faint shadow to the tooltip. That is a static widget difference, not a response.
- No pressed state, no highlight travel, no evidence of the rim shifting with pointer position
  anywhere in 22 shots.
- `glass-regular-dark-menu.png` is byte-for-byte a hero shot with **no menu open** — the one capture
  that might have shown a transient surface is empty.

## Additional defects worth fixing regardless of score

- **Text clipped mid-glyph at pane edges.** `glass-regular-dark-terminal.png` and
  `glass-regular-light-terminal.png`: line 21 `readonly name: string;` is sliced through its
  descenders by the panel's top edge, with the horizontal scrollbar overlapping the same row.
  `glass-regular-dark-palette.png`: the last row `Ports: Focus on Ports View` is cut mid-glyph by
  the panel bottom with no fade or mask.
- **Ghosted, colliding text.** `glass-clear-palette.png`, y ≈ 715–735: editor code
  (`): [T, (newProps: T) => void]`) shows *through* the palette and overprints
  `Output: Focus on Output View`. Two texts superimposed is a hard legibility failure.
- **Diff fills are opaque, over-saturated and asymmetric.** `glass-regular-dark-diff.png`: inserted
  lines `#355f3b` (L 77) on an editor of `#13161f` (L 22) — **+55 L**; deleted lines `#291c24`
  (L 32) — **+10 L**. Insert is 5.5× the weight of delete. The word-level box measures `#355e3b`
  against a line fill of `#355f3b`: **one unit apart, so the word-level information is invisible.**
  The inserted-line gutter block is maroon `#372025` sitting directly against a green line — a
  straight colour clash.
- **Merge conflict floods the editor.** `glass-regular-dark-notifications.png`: the Current Change
  block `#233d3f` covers lines 15–33 with a heavy teal wash topped by a very saturated `#3c7772`
  header line, and the minimap carries solid teal and blue bars. Against the toast's `#37557a` blue
  the combination is loud and unresolved.
- **Toast internals clash.** Header `#4c5d64` (desaturated grey-teal) against a body of `#37557a`
  (saturated blue) inside one widget, with a hard seam between them; the toast is jammed into the
  window's bottom-right corner with square outer corners and no margin.
- **Window edge is a dark vignette, not a rim.** At (3, 800) and (2396, 800) in the dark hero,
  `#0b0e16` (L 13.9) — darker than both the activity bar (33.7) and the editor (22.0). Apple's
  window edge carries the brightest hairline on screen.
- **Broken/mis-targeted captures.** `glass-regular-dark-menu.png` (no menu),
  `zoom-palette-rim-bottom-left.png` (2/3 black, contains no rim). Both were meant to be evidence.
- **Light-mode contrast.** Line numbers measure **4.12 : 1** against `#f9fafe` — below WCAG AA.
  Comments measure 4.52 : 1, marginal, and worse in practice on thin antialiased strokes.

## The 10 most valuable changes, in priority order

**1. Ship real edge lensing on the three overlay surfaces first (palette, notification, hover).**
Displace the backdrop with a radial/edge-normal ramp confined to the outer 12–16 px — strongest at
the boundary, decaying to zero by ~20 px in, with a slight magnification just inside the rim.
*Where it's visible:* `glass-regular-dark-palette.png` (code at x < 130 currently just stops);
`glass-regular-dark-hover.png` (`name: st` sliced at x ≈ 130).
*Expected effect:* the single change that converts "translucent panel" into "Liquid Glass"; a
designer reads it before they read anything else.
*Verify:* open the palette over a file containing a long unbroken horizontal rule or a wide box-drawing
line so it passes under the pane edge. Screenshot at 4×. The line must remain **continuous** and
visibly **kink/compress within 12 px of the rim**. Today it is cut. Also re-shoot
`zoom-palette-rim-bottom-left.png` so it actually contains the rim.

**2. Make the material transmit. The sidebar and editor currently do not.**
Effects on vs off changes the sidebar by ΔL 1.0 and the editor by ΔL 0.9. Raise transmission on the
sidebar, panel and overlays until real content is legible-but-blurred through them, then re-tune
text weight for the new ground.
*Where it's visible:* the layer1 vs effects comparison above; `glass-clear-hero.png`, where "Clear"
blocks the vivid ground it is supposed to reveal.
*Expected effect:* the variant axis (opaque / regular / clear) becomes a real product feature
instead of three near-identical files.
*Verify:* re-shoot `transparent-window-mode-cdp-capture.png` over a photographic wallpaper. A
recognisable blurred version of the wallpaper must be visible **through the sidebar body**, not just
in the gaps. Then diff regular-vs-clear sidebar crops — they must differ by more than 15 L.

**3. Re-aim and close the specular rim.**
Target a top-leading light: top ≈ leading > trailing > bottom, every edge non-zero, with the falloff
running smoothly *around the corner arc* rather than switching at the corner. Kill the mid-edge
brightness bump.
*Where it's visible:* palette edges measure +110 / +27 / +7 / +4 (L / T / B / R); the sidebar's left
rim peaks at y = 370 and is dimmest at the top.
*Expected effect:* panes stop reading as "left accent border" and gain a continuous lit outline —
the thing Apple's tab-bar pill (`11-…-dark.png`) has and this does not.
*Verify:* script a perimeter walk sampling rim luma every 10 px around a pane and plot it. Must be a
smooth single-peak curve, peak at the top-leading corner, minimum never below ~25 % of peak. Today
it is a four-step function with two near-zero sides.

**4. Establish a radius token scale and enforce concentricity.**
Sidebar/panel/editor ≈ 16–20 px, overlays ≈ 24–28 px, rows/pills = parent radius − inset. Give the
sidebar and the terminal panel actual corners.
*Where it's visible:* sidebar r = 0 (left edge sits at x = 92–96 for every row from y = 74 to
y = 1532); toast r ≈ 6 with r ≈ 14 buttons inside it; light palette's square full-bleed selected row
inside a 20 px parent.
*Expected effect:* removes the strongest "this is a web page, not a Mac app" signal.
*Verify:* 4× crop of all four corners of each pane plus one nested child, side by side. Every child
arc must be visibly parallel to its parent's, and no child radius may exceed its parent's.

**5. Replace every hard groove and banded edge with one soft inner shadow, and dial the outer
shadow up by ~3×.**
*Where it's visible:* terminal panel top — 8 px of near-black (L ≈ 12.5) then a bright line
(L 77), a bevel; the 10 px band inside the palette's left rim ending in a hard step at x = 134;
the four-value activity-bar/sidebar joint at x 62–67.
*Expected effect:* panes read as slabs with depth instead of rectangles with outlines; the
double-border artefacts disappear.
*Verify:* sample a 40 px perpendicular profile across each boundary. It must be **monotonic and
smooth** — no local minimum darker than ambient sitting next to a local maximum. The palette's
bottom shadow must extend ≥ 35 px and be visible at 100 % zoom without squinting.

**6. Derive foreground colour from the material (real vibrancy).**
Blend token colours toward the local backdrop and boost saturation, so the same token renders
differently on the L 44 sidebar and the L 22 editor. Make separators vibrant rather than flat rules.
*Where it's visible:* identical hex on both surfaces today; the flat Outline/Timeline dividers;
status-bar text unchanged between `glass-clear-hero.png` (`#143765` ground) and
`glass-opaque-layer1-hero.png` (`#1c2129` ground).
*Expected effect:* this is what Apple's figure 04 vs 05 pair exists to teach — it buys legibility
*and* the material reading simultaneously.
*Verify:* screenshot the same file in the editor and in a peek/sidebar preview; sample the same
token in both. The hex must differ, and both must keep ≥ 4.5 : 1 against their local ground.

**7. Rebuild the notification toast — currently the worst widget in the product.**
Remove the outward bright halo (L 124.9 → 162.5 outside a rim on an L 45 ground) and replace it with
a real dark shadow; drop the flat opaque `#37557a` for a translucent tinted material; unify the
`#4c5d64` header with the body; inset it ~16 pt from the window corner; radius up and make the
buttons concentric.
*Where it's visible:* `glass-regular-dark-notifications.png`, bottom-right.
*Expected effect:* removes the one element that actively signals "Windows Aero", and stops the toast
clashing with the merge-conflict teal behind it.
*Verify:* perpendicular scan outward from the toast's left rim must show luma **decreasing** below
ambient and recovering over ~40 px. Zero pixels outside the rim may be brighter than the interior.

**8. Rebalance diff and merge fills as translucent tints of equal perceptual weight.**
Insert is +55 L, delete is +10 L; the word-level box is one L unit from the line fill; the inserted-
line gutter is maroon under a green line.
*Where it's visible:* `glass-regular-dark-diff.png` lines 4+/13+/16−/18+/24+/27+;
`glass-regular-dark-notifications.png` merge block, lines 15–33.
*Expected effect:* the diff stops being the loudest thing on screen and stops overprinting the
material; word-level changes become readable again.
*Verify:* sample insert and delete fills — |ΔL| between them must be ≤ 8, and both ≤ ~25 above the
editor ground. The word-level box must be ≥ 15 L above its own line fill and carry a small radius.
The gutter marker hue must match its line's hue.

**9. Fix the light variant: give it separation, tint and legal contrast.**
Sidebar `#f3f7ff` vs editor `#f9fafe` is ΔL 3.4 (1.04 : 1). Line numbers are 4.12 : 1 — below AA.
There is no hue anywhere.
*Where it's visible:* `glass-regular-light-hero.png`, `…-light-terminal.png`.
*Expected effect:* the light variant currently reads as an unfinished draft; Apple's figure 01 and 08
both show light glass carrying obvious tint and obvious layering.
*Verify:* pane-to-pane ΔL ≥ 10 with a visible shadow between them; every text token ≥ 4.5 : 1 (≥ 3 : 1
for genuinely decorative marks) measured on the glyph core, not the antialias.

**10. Stop clipping and ghosting text at pane boundaries, and re-shoot the broken captures.**
Add bottom padding / a fade mask at scroll boundaries; raise material opacity or add a text scrim
where content shows through.
*Where it's visible:* `readonly name: string;` sliced by the terminal panel top in both dark and
light; `Ports: Focus on Ports View` cut by the palette bottom; `glass-clear-palette.png` y ≈ 715–735
where editor code overprints `Output: Focus on Output View`;
`glass-regular-dark-menu.png` contains no menu.
*Expected effect:* removes the "unfinished" read, which currently undercuts the polished parts.
*Verify:* no glyph in any capture may be intersected by a pane edge; no two text runs may overlap.
Re-capture the menu shot with a menu actually open.

## Overall verdict

A designer who knows Liquid Glass would recognise this as **a competent, legible, slightly
translucent dark VS Code theme — and would not recognise it as Liquid Glass.** They would reach that
conclusion in about three seconds, from the sidebar's square corners and the fact that the code
behind the command palette is cut off rather than bent. The material never bends light, never casts
